About Pricing

Pricing content objects give your application easy access to Pricing and Market Data. Pricing data refers to exchange-traded, contributed, and evaluated data for all financial assets, including :

  • commodities
  • derivatives
  • equities
  • fixed income
  • foreign exchange
  • funds
  • indices
  • loans used by financial market participants.

By using Pricing content objects, your application can retrieve this content as snapshots or as a stream of data that is capable of receiving updates with the latest values using callbacks.
